\. says “take the real dot.” \w+ says “take one or more word characters.” Together, they match .com.

For univ.dz, the TLD is dz. For example.co.uk, it is uk. Check the official TLD list ↗

These are guided visual explanations, not a live regex-engine debugger. Match positions and outputs were generated with Python’s re module. You do not need to read the whole page at once: each example is a short lesson.
